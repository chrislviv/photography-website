About

The Photographer

Behind the Lens

My journey into automotive photography began with a simple obsession: the way light moves across a body panel at dawn. What started as a curiosity became a discipline — learning to read machines the way a wildlife photographer reads an animal. Every car has behaviour, personality, and a decisive moment.

Based in Belgium, I shoot supercars, hypercars, and classic machines across Europe. My work spans private collections, track days, and editorial commissions — always chasing the image that makes you feel the machine, not just see it.

What drives my automotive photography is the belief that engineered objects carry as much emotion as living ones. A Pagani at golden hour is as much a portrait subject as any predator in the wild. Both require patience, technical precision, and the willingness to wait for the moment when everything aligns.

I shoot exclusively in manual mode. Full creative control over every frame — exposure decisions in fading track light, precise focus on a headlight reflection, the exact instant a car exits a corner with the right body lean. Nothing is left to chance.

My Approach

Automotive photography is 80% preparation. Understanding the light, the location, the car's proportions, the angle that makes a compact car look monumental — this all happens before the shutter fires.

Whether I'm at a private collection shoot in Belgium or a track day in Monaco, I approach every session with the same rigour: scout the location, plan for the available light, anticipate the car's movement, and find the angles others walk past.

My goal isn't to document machines. It's to create images that make you feel what it's like to stand next to something extraordinary.

Expertise & Focus

  • Supercar & hypercar photography
  • Classic and vintage automotive
  • Track day & motorsport coverage
  • Low-light & golden hour mastery

The Equipment

Tools of the Trade

The Equipment

Great automotive photography demands both artistic vision and technical precision. My equipment choices reflect years of research and real-world use across tracks, private estates, and city streets.

Primary System

Camera Body

Canon R5 Mark II

The core of my system. 45MP full-frame sensor, exceptional autofocus with subject tracking, and the low-light performance to handle underlit garages and dusk track sessions without compromise.

  • 45MP full-frame sensor
  • Advanced subject tracking AF
  • Up to 30fps electronic shutter
  • Outstanding low-light capability
Primary Lens

Canon RF 70-200mm f/2.8L

My most-used lens for automotive work. The compression it creates at 200mm makes cars look sculptural. Fast enough for moving subjects in any light, sharp enough to resolve every panel gap.

  • f/2.8 throughout the range
  • Exceptional sharpness wide open
  • Fast, silent autofocus
  • Weather-sealed construction
Wide Lens

Canon RF 24-70mm f/2.8L

For environmental context shots, tight garages, and the dramatic wide-angle perspectives that put a car in its surroundings. Essential for editorial work where location is part of the story.

  • Versatile everyday zoom
  • Constant f/2.8 aperture
  • Minimal distortion at 24mm
  • L-series build quality

Support & Accessories

Stability

Carbon Fibre Tripod

For long exposures, light trails, and any situation where absolute stability is non-negotiable.

Filters

ND & Polariser Set

Variable ND for controlling motion blur on moving cars. Polariser for eliminating reflections on glass and paint.

High-speed cards essential when shooting bursts of fast-moving cars. No bottlenecks, no missed moments.

Superior colour grading for automotive work. The tethering and colour control are unmatched for client shoots.
