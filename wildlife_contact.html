Commercial Collaboration

Brand Partnerships

I partner with brands that align with my values of conservation, sustainability, and authentic storytelling. My visual style brings a distinctive, editorial quality to commercial projects.

  • Sponsored content & social media campaigns
  • Product photography & brand imagery
  • Ambassador programs for outdoor/conservation brands
  • Editorial features & commissioned work
  • Gear testing & review collaborations

My audience values authenticity, technical excellence, and conservation-minded content. I work only with brands I genuinely believe in and would recommend to my community.

Fine Art & Commercial Use

Prints & Licensing

My wildlife images are available as limited edition fine art prints and for commercial licensing. Each print is museum-quality and ships with a certificate of authenticity.

  • Limited edition fine art prints
  • Multiple sizes & framing options
  • Commercial licensing available
  • Editorial & publishing rights
  • Custom print requests considered

All prints support ongoing conservation documentation work and are produced using archival-quality materials designed to last generations.

One-on-One Instruction

Photography Mentoring

I offer personalized mentoring for photographers looking to elevate their wildlife photography or prepare for safari trips. Drawing on my transition from automatic to manual mastery, I provide practical, actionable guidance.

  • 1-on-1 video consultation sessions
  • Portfolio review & feedback
  • Tanzania safari preparation & planning
  • Technical skills: manual mode, composition, light
  • Post-processing workflow guidance
  • Gear recommendations & optimization

Whether you're planning your first safari or wanting to refine your technique, I can help you capture images that truly showcase wildlife's soul.
