The Equipment

Tools of the Trade

Great wildlife photography demands both artistic vision and technical precision. My equipment choices reflect years of research, hands-on experience, and a commitment to capturing moments that others might miss.

Primary System

Camera Body

Canon R5 Mark II

The backbone of my wildlife photography system. This mirrorless powerhouse delivers exceptional autofocus performance and low-light capability essential for unpredictable safari conditions.

  • 45MP full-frame sensor
  • Advanced animal eye detection
  • Exceptional low-light performance
  • Professional build quality
Primary Lens

Canon RF 70-200mm f/2.8

My workhorse lens for wildlife photography. The versatile focal range and fast f/2.8 aperture provide stunning subject isolation and performance in golden hour light.

  • Professional f/2.8 aperture
  • Ideal for medium-range wildlife
  • Exceptional sharpness
  • Beautiful bokeh rendering

Supporting Equipment

Supporting Lens

Canon RF 600mm f/4

The telephoto specialist for distant subjects and elusive wildlife. When reach matters most, this lens delivers professional-grade sharpness and subject isolation at extreme distances.

  • Professional f/4 constant aperture
  • Essential for distant wildlife
  • Exceptional reach for shy subjects
  • Professional telephoto performance
Supporting Lens

Canon RF 24-105mm f/4-7.1

Versatile zoom for environmental wildlife portraits and habitat context. When the story requires showing the animal in its surroundings, this lens provides the wider perspective.

  • Flexible focal range
  • Ideal for environmental portraits
  • Lightweight and portable
  • Context and storytelling capability

Equipment Philosophy

Premium equipment enables the vision, but understanding light, behavior, and composition creates the image. My transition to shooting exclusively in manual mode wasn't about rejecting technology—it was about gaining complete creative authority over every technical decision in the field.

My gear choices reflect the demands of professional wildlife photography: fast glass for low light performance, reliable autofocus for unpredictable moments, and focal lengths that allow me to work at the distances wildlife requires. Each lens serves a specific purpose, chosen through real-world experience rather than spec sheets.

Technical excellence serves artistic vision. Every piece of equipment in my kit exists to help me capture the soul of wildlife—to freeze moments that reveal character, behavior, and beauty that might otherwise go unseen.
